* *Parable of the Lamp (4:21–25)*


* *Parable of the Growing Seed (4:26–29)*


* *Parable of the Mustard Seed (4:30–34)*


* *Jesus Calms the Storm (4:35–41)*

#43 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:15
Question: Quote Mark 4:15.
Answer:   "The seed that fell on the footpath represents those who hear the message, only to have Satan come at once and take it away."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#44 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:16–17
Question: Quote Mark 4:16–17.
Answer:   "The seed on the rocky soil represents those who hear the message and immediately receive it with joy. But since they don't have deep roots, they don't last long. They fall away as soon as they have problems or are persecuted for believing God's word."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#45 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:18–19
Question: Quote Mark 4:18–19.
Answer:   "The seed that fell among the thorns represents others who hear God's word, but all too quickly the message is crowded out by the worries of this life, the lure of wealth, and the desire for other things, so no fruit is produced."
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#46 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:20
Question: Quote Mark 4:20.
Answer:   "And the seed that fell on good soil represents those who hear and accept God's word and produce a harvest of thirty, sixty, or even a hundred times as much as had been planted!"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#50 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:35–41
Question: From the section titled *Jesus Calms the Storm*, identify the words spoken by Jesus to start the journey, the words spoken to the storm, and the two questions he asked his disciples afterward:
Answer:   * To his disciples (verse 35): "Let's cross to the other side of the lake."


* To the waves (verse 39): "Silence! Be still!"


* To his disciples (verse 40): "Why are you afraid? Do you still have no faith?"
