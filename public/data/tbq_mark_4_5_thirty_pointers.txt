#81 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:1-41
Question: List the five section headings and verse references found in Mark Chapter 4:
Answer:   * *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed (4:1–4:20)*
* *Parable of the Lamp (4:21–4:25)*
* *Parable of the Growing Seed (4:26–4:29)*
* *Parable of the Mustard Seed (4:30–4:34)*
* *Jesus Calms the Storm (4:35–4:41)*
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#82 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:3-8
Question: From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, describe the four locations where seed fell and the outcome for each:
Answer:   * The footpath: Seed fell and birds ate it.
* Shallow rocky soil: Seed sprouted quickly, but wilted and died in the hot sun without deep roots.
* Among thorns: Thorns grew up and choked the tender plants so they bore no grain.
* Fertile soil: Seeds sprouted, grew, and yielded thirty, sixty, and a hundred times what was planted.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#83 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:14-17
Question: From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, summarize Jesus's explanation of the footpath and rocky ground:
Answer:   * Footpath: Hearers listen to the message, but Satan arrives immediately and carries it off.
* Rocky soil: Hearers receive the word with joy right away, but fall away as soon as problems or persecution arise due to a lack of deep roots.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

#84 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:18-19
Question: From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, what three obstacles choke out the message in thorny ground?
Answer:   * The worries of this life.
* The lure of wealth.
* The desire for other things.

#85 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4:20

#100 | Points: 30 | Ref: M 4 & 5 Review
Question: Contrast the conclusions of the two major faith narratives across Mark 4 and 5 (the lake storm and the restoration of Jairus's daughter):
Answer:   * In the storm, the disciples moved from fear of perishing to awe as Jesus silenced the wind and waves with a command.
* In the home of Jairus, mourning and mocking laughter were transformed into overwhelming astonishment as Jesus took the dead child's hand and raised her to walk.
