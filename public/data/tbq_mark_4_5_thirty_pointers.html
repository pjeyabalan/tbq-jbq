MARK CHAPTER 4 (11 Questions)
#81 30 Pts M 4:1-41
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
List the five section headings and verse references found in Mark Chapter 4:
Answer: * *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed (4:1–4:20)* * *Parable of the Lamp (4:21–4:25)* * *Parable of the Growing Seed (4:26–4:29)* * *Parable of the Mustard Seed (4:30–4:34)* * *Jesus Calms the Storm (4:35–4:41)*
#82 30 Pts M 4:3-8
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, describe the four locations where seed fell and the outcome for each:
Answer: * The footpath: Seed fell and birds ate it. * Shallow rocky soil: Seed sprouted quickly, but wilted and died in the hot sun without deep roots. * Among thorns: Thorns grew up and choked the tender plants so they bore no grain. * Fertile soil: Seeds sprouted, grew, and yielded thirty, sixty, and a hundred times what was planted.
#83 30 Pts M 4:14-17
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, summarize Jesus's explanation of the footpath and rocky ground:
Answer: * Footpath: Hearers listen to the message, but Satan arrives immediately and carries it off. * Rocky soil: Hearers receive the word with joy right away, but fall away as soon as problems or persecution arise due to a lack of deep roots.
#84 30 Pts M 4:18-19
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, what three obstacles choke out the message in thorny ground?
Answer: * The worries of this life. * The lure of wealth. * The desire for other things.
#85 30 Pts M 4:20
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Farmer Scattering Seed*, how does Jesus define the good ground in verse 20?
Answer: Those who hear and welcome God's word, bearing a harvest of thirty, sixty, or a hundred times what was sown.
#86 30 Pts M 4:24-25
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Lamp*, summarize the instruction on listening given in verses 24 and 25:
Answer: Paying close attention results in more understanding being granted, while failing to listen causes even what little insight someone holds to be taken away.
#87 30 Pts M 4:28
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Growing Seed*, what three sequential stages of crop growth are described in verse 28?
Answer: * First, a leaf blade pushes through. * Then, heads of wheat develop. * Finally, the grain fully ripens.
#88 30 Pts M 4:31-32
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Parable of the Mustard Seed*, contrast the initial planting size with the mature plant:
Answer: It begins as the smallest of all seeds in the earth, but develops into the largest garden plant with broad branches where birds can nest.
#89 30 Pts M 4:37-38
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Calms the Storm*, describe the condition of the boat and Jesus's actions during the squall:
Answer: High waves broke over the vessel until it took on water, while Jesus rested asleep on a cushion in the back of the boat.

#100 30 Pts M 4 & 5 Review
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
Contrast the conclusions of the two major faith narratives across Mark 4 and 5 (the lake storm and the restoration of Jairus's daughter):
Answer: * In the storm, the disciples moved from fear of perishing to awe as Jesus silenced the wind and waves with a command. * In the home of Jairus, mourning and mocking laughter were transformed into overwhelming astonishment as Jesus took the dead child's hand and raised her to walk.
MARK CHAPTER 5 (9 Questions)
#90 30 Pts M 5:3-5
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man*, describe the man's wild strength and solitary behavior:
Answer: He lived among burial caves, broke chains from his wrists and smashed shackles without anyone being strong enough to subdue him, and wandered hills night and day howling and striking himself with rocks.

TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man*, detail the conversation regarding identity and destination:
Answer: The spirit declared its name was Legion because many were inside, and begged not to be sent away to a distant place, asking instead to enter a nearby herd of pigs.
#92 30 Pts M 5:13-17
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man*, describe the fate of the pigs and the reaction of the surrounding residents:
Answer: The herd of about 2,000 pigs plunged down the steep hill into the lake and drowned; herdsmen spread the report, leading fearful crowds to plead with Jesus to leave.
#93 30 Pts M 5:18-20
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man*, contrast the delivered man's desire with Jesus's instructions:
Answer: The man begged to journey in the boat with Jesus, but Jesus commanded him to return to his family to share the great mercy the Lord had shown, prompting him to speak across the Ten Towns.
#94 30 Pts M 5:25-26
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals in Response to Faith*, summarize the twelve-year struggle of the woman in verses 25 and 26:
Answer: She had suffered from continual bleeding for twelve years, endured treatments under many doctors, spent all her money, and had only grown worse instead of improving.
#95 30 Pts M 5:27-33
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals in Response to Faith*, describe the steps leading from the woman's touch to her confession:
Answer: Believing touching his robe would heal her, she touched it and felt restored; Jesus noticed healing power leave him and asked who touched him, leading the trembling woman to kneel and tell the whole story.
#96 30 Pts M 5:35-36
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals in Response to Faith*, describe the exchange between the messengers, Jesus, and Jairus:
Answer: Messengers arrived reporting the daughter was dead and advised against bothering the Teacher; Jesus overheard (or ignored) this report and told Jairus not to fear, but simply have faith.
#97 30 Pts M 5:38-40
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals in Response to Faith*, contrast the behavior of the mourners before and after Jesus commented on the child:
Answer: They were weeping and wailing with much commotion, but when Jesus stated the girl was merely asleep, they laughed at him, leading him to put them all outside.
#98 30 Pts M 5:41-42
TBQ Mark 4 & 5 100-Drill Set
From the section titled *Jesus Heals in Response to Faith*, describe the raising of the young girl and the response of those present:
Answer: Jesus took her hand and said "Talitha koum" ("Little girl, get up!"), and the twelve-year-old girl stood up and walked, leaving the onlookers completely overwhelmed and amazed.
