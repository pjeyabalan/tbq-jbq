From the section titled Parable of the Growing Seed, what three successive stages does the earth produce on its own in verse 28?
Answer: First a leaf blade pushes through, then the heads of wheat are formed, and finally the grain ripens.
#10 30 Pts M 4:35-40
TBQ Mark 4 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Calms the Storm, identify the words spoken by Jesus to start the journey, the words spoken to the storm, and the two questions he asked his disciples afterward.
Answer: To disciples (v. 35): "Let's cross to the other side of the lake." / To storm (v. 39): "Silence! Be still!" / To disciples (v. 40): "Why are you afraid? Do you still have no faith?"
MARK CHAPTER 5 (10 Questions)
#11 30 Pts M 5:4-5
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man, describe the man's extraordinary strength and self-harming behavior in verses 4 and 5.
Answer: He easily snapped metal chains off his wrists and shattered leg irons, defying all human restraint; day and night he wandered among tombs and hills, shouting loudly and slashing his body with sharp stones.
#12 30 Pts M 5:10-13
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man, trace the interaction between the evil spirits and Jesus regarding their destination across verses 10–13.
Answer: The spirits pleaded repeatedly not to be banished out of the territory; seeing a herd of pigs on the hillside, they asked permission to enter the swine, and upon Jesus's consent, the entire herd of roughly 2,000 stampeded into the lake and drowned.
#13 30 Pts M 5:18-20
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals a Demon-Possessed Man, contrast the healed man's personal plea with the assignment Jesus gave him in verses 18–20.
Answer: The delivered man pleaded to travel in the boat with Jesus, but Jesus directed him to go home to his family and recount how merciful God had been, leading the man to proclaim it across the Ten Towns.
#14 30 Pts M 5:26
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, detail the medical and financial hardships the woman endured as described in verse 26.
Answer: She had suffered terribly under the care of multiple physicians over twelve years, spent all her financial resources paying them, and instead of recovering had only grown worse.
#15 30 Pts M 5:29-33
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, describe the sequence of events from the woman's touch to her confession in verses 29–33.
Answer: Upon touching his robe, the bleeding ceased immediately; Jesus felt power depart and questioned who touched him, leading the trembling woman to come forward, fall to her knees, and confess the whole truth.
#16 30 Pts M 5:34
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, what three things did Jesus speak to reassure the trembling woman in verse 34?
Answer: He called her "Daughter" and confirmed that her faith had restored her, told her to go in peace, and declared that her suffering was finished.
#17 30 Pts M 5:35-36
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, describe the exchange between the messengers from Jairus's house and Jesus's response to Jairus in verses 35 and 36.
Answer: Household messengers arrived reporting the girl was already dead and urged Jairus not to bother the Teacher; Jesus overheard (or dismissed) their words and immediately told Jairus to cast off fear and simply believe.
#18 30 Pts M 5:38-40
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, contrast the behavior of the household mourners before and after Jesus's comment about the child's status in verses 38–40.
Answer: The house was originally filled with noisy commotion, weeping, and wailing; when Jesus told them the girl was not dead but merely sleeping, the mourners shifted from weeping to laughing at him, leading Jesus to send them all outside.
#19 30 Pts M 5:40
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, list the five individuals who accompanied Jesus into the room where the deceased girl lay in verse 40.
Answer: The young girl's father (Jairus), the young girl's mother, Peter, James, and John.
#20 30 Pts M 5:41-43
TBQ Mark 5 Practice Set
From the section titled Jesus Heals in Response to Faith, describe the miracle, the crowd's reaction, and Jesus's final commands across verses 41–43.
Answer: Jesus took her hand and commanded "Talitha koum", causing the twelve-year-old child to walk; onlookers were overwhelmed with wonder, and Jesus gave strict orders not to tell anyone while instructing them to give her food.
