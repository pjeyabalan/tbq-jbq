⚡ Official JBQ Scoring Rules

  • • 20 Questions: Official match consists of questions 1 through 20.
  • • Point Values: 10, 20, or 30 points.
  • • Quiz Out: 6 correct answers = Quiz Out (+10 bonus if 0 errors).
  • • Errored Out: 3 errors = Errored Out for regulation.
  • • Incorrect Answers: -5 on 10-pointers, -10 on 20-pointers, -15 on 30-pointers (irrespective of interruption).
  • • Interruption Error: Re-Read rebound for opposite team!
